100% On-Device Privacy • Zero Data Collection

Privacy Policy for Wird

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Wird — Spiritual Habit & Mamulat Tracker ("Wird", "we", "our", or " the App") is built on a strict privacy-first architecture. Your spiritual routines, daily prayer logs, dhikr counts, and menstrual/excuse cycle records belong solely to you.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Wird does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal information, usage telemetry, health records, or device identifiers. No user account or registration is required to use the App.

2. 100% Local On-Device Storage

All data entered into Wird—including custom habits, 31-day Mamulat matrix logs, Lataif/Dhikr counters, journal reflections, and Intelligent Period & Excuse Mode cycle dates—is stored exclusively on your device using local browser/WebView storage (IndexedDB and local preferences).

3. Health & Cycle Tracking Privacy

The optional Period & Excuse Mode (Uzr/Hayd tracker) calculates cycle phases and prayer/fasting exemptions locally on your device. Cycle dates and phase calculations never leave your device.

4. Data Backup, Export & Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • You can export your logs locally as JSON or Excel spreadsheets using the in-app Export feature.
  • You can permanently erase all data at any time via Settings → Reset / Clear Data or by uninstalling the App.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

Wird contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no cross-app tracking technologies.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Support Page or open an issue at github.com/mbany786/wird.
