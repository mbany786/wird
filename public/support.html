Frequently Asked Questions

How do I edit counts directly on the 31-Day Matrix tab?

Use the Active Edit Day bar at the top of the Matrix to select any day (1–31), then tap the inline [−] and [+] buttons directly inside any habit row or tap any cell in the grid to open the Floating Matrix Quick-Count Dock.

How does the Intelligent Period & Excuse Mode work?

Open Cycle & Excuse Mode from the top navigation bar to log your last period start date, cycle length, and period duration. Wird automatically calculates your current hormonal phase (Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory, or Luteal), highlights expected moods and wellness tips, and can automatically mark ritual prayers as excused (Uzr) while keeping dhikr and spiritual reflection active.

How do I create a blank custom routine or switch templates?

Tap the Templates button in the header to choose from curated spiritual routines, start from a Blank Custom Template, or generate a personalized habit set.

How do I back up or transfer my data?

Because Wird stores 100% of your data privately on your device, you can export a full JSON or Excel backup anytime from the top toolbar and import it on another device.
